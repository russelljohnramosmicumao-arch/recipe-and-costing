How costing works

Purchase price ÷ purchase quantity = base-unit cost. Recipe quantity is converted to the ingredient's base unit when needed. Unknown conversions are deliberately left unresolved.

Imported source notes

The menu document contains several internal conflicts (for example, ingredient-list quantities vs procedure quantities). Those recipes are marked Needs review instead of being silently corrected.
